---
name: World art direction
description: The user's visual expectations for the Survivor camp and boundaries of Outlaster inspiration.
---

Prioritize the actual playable world: cohesive coastal terrain, crafted landmark
silhouettes, varied tropical foliage, inhabited detail, and a readable survivor.
Do not substitute menu/HUD redesign, recoloring, or more scattered generic shapes
for visible world quality. Outlaster is an inspiration for exploration and appeal,
not a source of models, proprietary art, or Roblox code.

**Why:** The user played the early version and explicitly rejected its flat
rectangular lawn and scattered primitive boxes/cones as amateurish, asking for a
major premium-quality environment pass.

**How to apply:** Inspect actual gameplay renders at the landmarks and default
spawn before judging visual work complete. Preserve walkability, phone controls,
landmark sightlines, and iPhone rendering constraints as the environment changes.
