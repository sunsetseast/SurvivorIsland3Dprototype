# Free 3D production strategy

[Start here](README.md) · [Art direction](ART_DIRECTION.md) · [Model audit](MODEL_AUDIT.md)

The recommended future method is manual Blender likeness sculpting, groom and wardrobe authoring on a free topology foundation, followed by binding to the existing Survivor skeleton and clips. The artist owns face structure, the body silhouette and all signature details. A free generator is useful for neutral topology and weight seeds. The previously rendered studies show how much likeness remains to be authored after a usable base mesh is available. [Blender studies](../cast-art-renaissance/README.md)

## Options within a zero-dollar tool budget

| Method | Availability and provenance | Use and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blender plus Blender Studio Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 | Official download lists the bundle as CC0. Confirm the ZIP and license against the pinned project provenance. An older Blender Studio training mesh is labeled CC BY, so identify the exact download. [Official CC0 bundle listing](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#assets) · [Older training mesh](https://studio.blender.org/training/realistic-human-research/use-of-base-meshes/) | Prefer the CC0 bundle as a proportion and sculpt base when its topology suits the subject. The PR #34 study path demonstrates offline availability, but the study meshes need production retopology or transfer, skin weights and independent face sculpting. |
| MakeHuman core or MPFB | MakeHuman Community lists core assets as CC0, MakeHuman code as AGPL and MPFB code as GPL. Blender Extensions lists MPFB support for Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Inspect the license of any add-on asset separately. [MakeHuman license](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md) · [MPFB license](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md) · [MPFB on Blender Extensions](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/) | Use core assets for rig-space fitting and a clean base topology. Use parameters for initial body block only. Hand sculpt jaw, lids, brow, nose, mouth and ear structure, then independently author clothing and groom. |
| Current GLBs, Ara and MakeHuman foundation | The repository records pinned MakeHuman and Ara revisions and clip provenance, including CMU motions. Keep applicable notices. [PR #29 provenance](../character-renaissance/FINAL_REPORT.md) · [PR #31 binder notes](../../tools/character/blender/README.md) | Lowest risk for bind-space, toe contacts, animation and Three.js behavior. Retain proven rig infrastructure and accepted garment components when appropriate. Avoid editing the existing baked face by parameter changes alone. |
| Blender and locally authored materials | Blender supports glTF export for skins, skeletal animations and standard PBR textures. Node-only effects may require baking. [Blender glTF export manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/scene_gltf2.html) | Author cloth, roughness, stubble, groom atlases and visible tattoo artwork from original drawing. Use geometry or masked cards for hair and hand-paint directional detail. Review transparency, texture seams and material count in Three.js. |
| Rigify or MPFB rigs and retargeting | Rigify is an optional Blender rigging tool. MPFB lists multiple rig choices. [Blender Rigify manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigify/basics.html) · [MPFB](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/) | Use a control rig for offline work only if useful. Prefer skinning new geometry to the existing runtime skeleton. If retargeting is required, explicitly map bone names and orientations and bake to the shipping deform rig. Validate feet and hands, root displacement, bind matrices and all clip samples. |

Source photographs are review references. Future public distribution requires its own decision on likeness, third-party branding and tattoo art. Base-mesh license and provenance are separate from those questions.

## Proposed production sequence after appearance approval

1. **Lock references.** Approve one coherent outfit and hair state per character. Collect the missing profile, rear and garment details that affect likeness or silhouette. Record the exact episode and keep variants separate.
2. **Review body and face studies.** Work in Blender with one consistent neutral light rig. Compare two contrasting subjects in the first production check, such as Cirie and Carolyn. Cirie tests natural body mass and garment fit. Carolyn tests mature facial structure, long hair and asymmetric ink. Keep Ozzy’s current asset as a known technical control.
3. **Gate art before rigging.** Review gray face front, three-quarter and profile, hair silhouette, clothed standing silhouette and two expression tests. Use side-by-side links or a review document viewable on iPhone. Record approval and corrections independently for face, build, hair, outfit and accessories.
4. **Bind approved geometry.** Preserve the existing 66-joint rest skeleton, inverse bind contract, named clips and toe bones. Weight-transfer only where body topology and rest-space alignment permit it. Correct shoulders, seat and knee poses by hand. The current binder shows a path that remaps joint palettes and preserves animation samples. [Binder](../../tools/character/blender/bind.py) · [Model registry](../../artifacts/survivor-camp-prototype/src/game/character/survivorModels.ts)
5. **Export one approved candidate.** Use the existing Meshopt and WebP route. Validate the decoded glTF, joint weights, clip names, animation samples and asset budgets. Inspect picker, camp, crawl, climb, balance and council sit under actual game lighting. Preserve season IDs and save behavior. [Asset loader](../../artifacts/survivor-camp-prototype/src/game/character/modelAssets.ts) · [Cast validation](../../artifacts/survivor-camp-prototype/scripts/validate-cast.mjs)
6. **Measure on the target device before scaling.** Collect physical iPhone Safari landscape captures and frame timing during solo play and a crowded council. Compare sustained frame pacing, material primitives, texture memory estimates and actual visual readability against baseline. Scale in reviewed batches until all 18 pass the same art and runtime gates.

The practical constraint is sculpt and review time per contestant. A complete rebuild should start only once at least two different face and body types pass likeness review and an approved candidate passes the rig and physical-device gates.
