## Appearance choice

Her winning season gives a distinctly readable outfit with the purple Malakal cloth around the torso. Anchor to the early camp outfit, with the face and waist-wrap placement both visible in broadcast footage.

Heroes vs. Villains has the yellow swim set in the opening challenge with a separate color and hair presentation. Keep it distinct from Malakal. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s20/images/e1r/e1tv29.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep the tapered lower face, lifted cheek line, strong brows, slightly upturned eye silhouette and a small asymmetric corner smile. The mouth stretches substantially in a full smile, so use the quieter C frame to set resting contours.

## Hair and grooming

Long deep brown hair, relatively straight with loose bends when released. A shows the hanging face-framing length. B shows it pulled up and back in camp. Keep the tie practical and the hairline natural, with some strands released in the loose state.

## Body and posture

Lean athletic build with a narrow torso and light shoulders, natural waist-to-hip transition and mobile long limbs. Preserve the relaxed, slightly angled standing posture. Keep the rib-to-waist shape credible underneath the wrap.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Black-and-light animal-print halter swim straps above the purple patterned Malakal Buff worn as a fitted torso tube, with pink short bottoms in B. D confirms the Buff construction and visible swim layers in a separate challenge state with a light cap and printed bottoms. The cap is optional for that state only.

## Tattoos and accessories

Small wrist details in B are present but too small for hardware identification. Exact pink shorts closure and any tattoo inventory are open. Preserve the Buff’s broad pattern blocks without inventing text.

## Modeling priorities

Read the wrap placement, layered halter straps and contrasting pink bottoms before tiny print. Make the cheek-to-mouth relationship recognizable with the hair hidden. Verify cloth and upper-arm clearance in climbing.

## Existing model corrections

The stock teal halter, gray-brown shorts and simple swept hair omit the purple torso wrap and layered animal print. The face and body are close to the generic fitted template. Correct the cheek, lid and smile anatomy, dark hair length and wrap-and-shorts silhouette.
