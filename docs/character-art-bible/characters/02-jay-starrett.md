## Face and expression

Keep prominent cheeks above a compact lower face with a gently tapered chin. Dark brows, close-hooded eyes at rest and the wide smile with deep outer creases are useful together. Compare B and C so the laugh does not determine the resting jaw.

## Hair and grooming

Dark brown, wavy and tousled, long enough to curl behind the ears and fall toward the nape. The Buff pushes it off the forehead in B. Tied-back hair is seen in the CBS interview. Facial hair reads light to clean in these frames.

## Body and posture

Lean, compact athletic outline with visible shoulder tone and a fairly narrow waist. Preserve the relaxed, slightly pitched-forward seated posture and animated smile, with ordinary thigh and upper-arm volumes.

## Tattoos and accessories

Black knit beanie is an arrival option, not part of the Buff look. No tattoo callout is supported by the reviewed frames. Footwear for the tank state needs a wider clothed view.

## Modeling priorities

Prioritize red and light pocket contrast, the split outline of waves behind the Buff and his cheek-led grin. Keep the patch pocket as a shallow raised panel with its seam.

## Existing model corrections
