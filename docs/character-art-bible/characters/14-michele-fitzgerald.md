## Face and expression

Deep brown hair swept back close and tied into a low bun in the early camp frames. A shows the scalp flow with headband in an arrival layer. Preserve irregular temple wisps and avoid a long hanging ponytail for the selected state.

## Body and posture

## Modeling priorities

The yellow must remain distinctly brighter and cooler than the existing ochre. Use fabric silhouette and broken dark/light pattern at mobile distance. Distinguish her cheek and angular chin from the Parvati base.

## Existing model corrections

Production mutes yellow to ochre, uses teal shorts and a tall-boot silhouette, and overweights accessory hoops. The pulled-back dark hair is a useful starting cue. Build a bun, distinctive cheeks and chin, plus bright cami and draping patterned trousers.
