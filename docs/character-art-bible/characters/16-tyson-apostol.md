Original roster season is **Tocantins**. [Original 2D portrait](https://github.com/sunsetseast/SurvivorIsland2025/blob/cf894e578d77fce1e588d793dfc8d14f61ec7628/Assets/Avatars/tyson.jpeg)

## Appearance choice

His winning season combines the recognizable longer tied hair, bold lime panel and Golden Galang palette. Tocantins provides a younger face with a shorter crown silhouette and different clothes.

Tocantins opening confessional has a younger, shorter styled crown and purple tee. Keep that costume separate. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s18/images/e1/e1tv10.jpg)

## Face and expression

Use a long narrow face, high pronounced bridge, angular cheeks, deeper upper lids and a relatively long lower face. Keep the uneven mouth corners in his dry half-smile. His profile should read before hair or Buff.

## Hair and grooming

Sandy blond to light brown pulled back into a low tie, with a narrow crown and loose side strands. In B the Buff pushes the hair back. Light facial stubble varies. Heavy beard and fully loose shoulder-length side curtains lack support for this opening look.

## Body and posture

Tall and spare with long limbs, relatively narrow chest and hips and understated shoulder mass. Preserve the high eye line and a relaxed slouch or hip shift. Avoid thick forearms or torso width added for a generic athlete.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Gray-green camouflage scoop tank with vivid yellow-green neck and arm binding and a large lime horizontal lower panel. A shows blue denim beneath it. B shows the golden-yellow and black patterned Galang forehead Buff. Exact jeans shade and hem need a closer full-body shot. A and B are both opening sequence views with the same tank.

## Tattoos and accessories

Buff weave and patch-scale print are documented, while tank branding and tattoo or jewelry inventory remain unverified. Dark sunglasses appear in the preseason interview as a separate state.

## Modeling priorities

Keep neck and torso narrow in profile. Use the lime panel and low tie to break a straight column silhouette. Design Buff folds to reveal enough brow for the long nose-and-eye relationship.

## Existing model corrections

Production uses a solid muted green sleeveless top, brown shorts and straight curtain-like blond hair under an orange band. Body reads broader and shorter through the legs than the footage. Refit tall narrow proportions and long face, use low tie, patterned Galang Buff and the lime-panel tank.
