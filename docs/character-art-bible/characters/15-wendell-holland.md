## Appearance choice

The Ghost Island winner’s woodworking confessional provides a cohesive camp outfit and useful face reference. His later Winners at War beard and clothing read differently.

Winners at War shows a fuller beard and different wardrobe. Keep that facial hair state separate. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s40/images/e0/e0tv103.jpg)

## Face and expression

Build an oval head with a pronounced upper cheek plane, full lips, clearly shaped jaw and a broad but natural nose base. The smile lifts both cheeks and folds the lower lids. Use A for a speaking rest reference and B for more visible nose and mouth volume.

## Hair and grooming

Dense dark tight curls with an irregular rounded crown and shorter perimeter, not a flat braided cap. A shows a close connected beard and mustache, with lighter cheeks and slightly denser chin and jaw.

## Body and posture

Lean athletic frame with strong shoulders and forearms but a trim waist and natural chest. Use D for the open torso in motion and A for a relaxed, grounded seated pose. Avoid a massive chest or squared-off arms.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Royal or cobalt blue plain short-sleeve crew tee and light wash jeans in A, with purple-blue Naviti Buff at the wrist and dark stacked bracelets on the other wrist. B shows the Buff at the neck in a separate shirtless challenge look. Keep white footwear or logos unverified for the camp kit.

## Tattoos and accessories

Bracelet and cloth wrist placements are legible in A. Exact bracelet bead construction and tattoo inventory need closer views. No standalone tattoo design was verified.

## Modeling priorities

Read the dark uneven curl perimeter, close beard and blue/denim contrast together. Use dark roughness variation inside curl clusters and make a clean neckline without a rigid purple collar.

## Existing model corrections

The production dark rust sleeveless top, olive cargo shorts and neck wrap miss the cobalt-and-denim camp state. Low, banded curl volume and dense chin geometry obscure the actual groom. Raise the irregular curl crown, paint a short beard boundary and fit the tee and jeans.
