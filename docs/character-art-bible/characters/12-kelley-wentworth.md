## Appearance choice

The Cambodia idol sequence gives Kelley a documented recognizable outfit and two hair states. The green Ta Keo cloth and aqua top are stronger identifiers for this appearance than the blue Manu layers or the S29 outfit.

Edge of Extinction has a blue Manu palette and a different hair-and-top combination. San Juan del Sur stays the original roster identity. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s38/images/e1/e1tv24.jpg)

## Face and expression

Use a narrow-to-moderate cheek span with pronounced cheekbones, fine chin, straight nose and a clearly shaped lower lip. Preserve the lid and brow tension in close-up without baking the concentrated expression into the neutral head.

## Hair and grooming

Long straight light blonde with slightly darker roots. Hair is loose past the shoulders in A, and gathered high above the forehead cloth in B and C. Keep the gathered lengths visible behind the head where supported and avoid a uniform yellow wedge.

## Body and posture

Lean long-limbed frame with defined shoulder lines and ordinary waist and thigh volume. Use C to set stance and relative limb length. Keep her posture focused and naturally upright rather than pin-up stylized.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Aqua V or softly scooped thin-strap tank over a black underlayer with visible straps, fitted black short bottoms and green patterned Ta Keo Buff. B documents forehead placement. C gives the same outfit colors at the challenge. Purple plaid overshirt is an arrival layer in the alternate S31 opening scenes.

## Tattoos and accessories

The green Buff and visible black under-straps carry the design. Verify the shoe model, tiny pocket or waist seams, and any jewelry or tattoo in a closer uncropped angle.

## Modeling priorities

Keep the strap stack at the shoulder and the blue-green-to-black color contrast. Use individually shaped cheeks and chin to separate her portrait from Andrea’s. Check the tank against a raised arm for clipping.

## Existing model corrections

Production has a solid navy tank, orange forehead cloth, green cargos and broad yellow hair planes. The repeated facial base obscures the cheekbone and narrow chin. Change the tank, black shorts and green Buff as a set and author both gathered and loose groom readings.
