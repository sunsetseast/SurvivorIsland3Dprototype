## Appearance choice

Samoa has a clear full standing outfit and close views of his recognizable hat, shaved head and chin-centered beard. Anchor to early Foa Foa, with the darker Heroes vs. Villains wardrobe and heavier beard kept as a later option.

Heroes vs. Villains shows a darker hat and fuller facial hair, with red Villains clothing visible in the S20 gallery. Treat it as a separate period. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s20/images/e0/e0tv85.jpg)

## Face and expression

Use a broad lower face, low brow, deep-set lids, a compact nose and pronounced jaw under the chin. Preserve the uneven mouth set and weight around the jaw. Read face volume in the hatless confessional, since the brim shadows his eyes.

## Hair and grooming

Balding or very closely shaved scalp with subtle dark growth. In the Samoa opening footage facial hair concentrates in a short goatee or chin patch with lighter mustache and cheek shadow. Avoid the long full beard from the existing asset.

## Body and posture

Compact and stocky, with thick torso, substantial upper arms, naturally rounded midsection and shorter-looking limbs. The relaxed stance sits low. Keep visible weight and avoid bodybuilding chest and abdominal definition.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Dark gray-brown short-brim pinched fedora, light stone long-sleeve button-front shirt with chest pocket, rolled cuffs, pale gray short bottoms and light running shoes. A and B show the hat, shirt and yellow Foa Foa cloth at the wrist. Read the wrinkles and relaxed shirt fit from B. Exact gray shorts fastening and shoe make are unverified.

## Tattoos and accessories

Hat profile and yellow wrist Buff are the signature accessories. No extensive tattoo coverage is supported by the visible areas. Keep any small ink, jewelry or neckline cord unverified until a clear S19 shot.

## Modeling priorities

The low fedora, low-set brow and compact body must read even if the face is small. Keep short beard density around the chin, and leave the shirt room to move over the torso.

## Existing model corrections

Production uses a long dark beard, purple-blue forehead wrap, black tank and generic cargo shorts. The frame reads slimmer and more upright than the source, and its beard overwhelms jaw shape. Rebuild the stocky proportions and chin patch, then fit the field shirt, short brim and yellow wrist Buff.
