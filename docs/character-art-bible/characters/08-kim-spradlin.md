## Appearance choice

Her winning season has a clear beach look in the idol sequence. The blue swim top and dark bottoms are directly visible together. The yellow arrival blouse and geometric jewelry provide a distinct same-season option.

Winners at War shows an older face, highlighted ponytail and red neck Buff with a pink layer. Record separately from One World. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s40/images/e0/e0tv114.jpg)

## Face and expression

Long face, clean high cheek planes and narrowing jaw toward a rounded pointed chin. Use the quiet, slightly turned angle to place nose and orbit. The expansive smile raises both cheek apples and changes the lower lid line.

## Hair and grooming

Long brown hair with sun-lightened brown lengths. B shows hair gathered with a loose over-shoulder fall. A shows loose swept hair and a longer side silhouette. Keep this spectrum rather than a single heavy straight shell.

## Body and posture

Tall, lean frame with long legs and a narrow torso. Keep light natural muscle and visible collarbone landmarks without exaggerated waist taper or fashion-model posing. B provides a hand-on-hip weight shift.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Deep-V saturated blue swim top with broad shoulder straps and black low-rise swim shorts from B. D adds a standing challenge silhouette. The mustard-gold sleeveless gathered blouse with dangling turquoise-centered geometric earrings and fine necklace belongs to the arrival outfit. It is separate from the primary swim set.

## Tattoos and accessories

The geometric earring shape is clear in A. Small jewelry and Buff placement in the primary swim set are unverified. No tattoo geometry is authorized by these views.

## Modeling priorities

Distinguish Kim from other lean women through face length, shoulder-to-hip spacing and long limbs. Fit the V and black shorts as two distinct fabric masses, with layered brown hair and no generic boots.

## Existing model corrections

The long brown silhouette is a starting point but the stock blue tank, loose cargo shorts and boots replace the documented beach outfit. Differentiate the longer cheek-to-chin structure, reduce shell-like hair volume and fit low-rise black shorts.
