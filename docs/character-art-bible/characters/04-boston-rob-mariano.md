# Boston Rob Mariano

[Open annotated photos](../references/04-boston-rob-mariano.md) · [Cast matrix](../CAST_MATRIX.md)

**Game ID 4** · **Season 22 · Redemption Island** · **Age about 35**

Original roster season is **Marquesas**. [Original 2D portrait](https://github.com/sunsetseast/SurvivorIsland2025/blob/cf894e578d77fce1e588d793dfc8d14f61ec7628/Assets/Avatars/bostonrob.jpeg)

## Appearance choice

His winning season has repeated clear views of the dark cap with outlined red Boston B, black tank and gold chain. It satisfies the Boston identity without copying the red cap from the 2D portrait or defaulting to Marquesas.

All-Stars already shows the dark Boston cap and a younger face. Use as an alternate period only. The Marquesas identity in the roster remains intact. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s8/images/e1/e1tv87.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep the broad cheek and jaw span, substantial lower face and rounded chin. Preserve the small lid opening under the brim, mild nose projection and smile that compresses the eyes. Use the open daylight face for neutral geometry and the side view to align ears and brim.

## Hair and grooming

Short dark hair mostly hidden by the cap. Fine dark mustache and close chin patch or goatee. Keep cheek coverage sparse, with the lip line visible.

## Body and posture

Sturdy and broad-shouldered with a thicker neck and torso than Ozzy or Tyson. Preserve weight through the middle and thighs and relaxed rounded shoulders in the camp pose. Avoid a narrow waist under inflated deltoids.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

A and B show a dark navy-to-charcoal curved-brim Red Sox cap with a red B outlined in pale thread, black scoop-neck tank and gold chain with round pendant. Use olive/khaki cargo trousers for the early beach state in C. The open gray field shirt and orange Ometepe wrist cloth are optional layers from B. Red/dark swim shorts in the idol-hunt sequence are a separate S22 state.

## Tattoos and accessories

Cap mark and pendant are signature. Keep the contour of the B legible in character selection subject to separate logo clearance for public release. Tattoo inventory and embroidery thread dimensions remain unverified.

## Modeling priorities

A three-quarter portrait must show the eye line beneath the brim, broad jaw and short goatee. Separate faded cotton cap, matte jersey and restrained metal. Keep the cap dark under warm light.

## Existing model corrections

Production uses a red cap without a readable B, oversized beard, narrow middle and short green cargos. The study shortens facial hair and adds an embroidered cue but keeps the red crown, narrow face and over-fitted black silhouette. Set the cap silhouette and mark from A, rebuild the broad jaw and stockier torso, add chain and fit the S22 trousers.
