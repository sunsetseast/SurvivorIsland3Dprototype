## Appearance choice

His winning season has clear views of the bald head, connected beard and Bayon colors. The dark navy or near-black fire-service T-shirt is the camp default, with the shirtless idol/confessional state available within the same season.

San Juan del Sur has the blue Hunahpu Buff and a younger face in the archive intro. Use it as an alternate alongside his S29 wardrobe. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s29/images/e2/e2tv24.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep a broad cheek and jaw structure with rounded chin support, a wide nose base and a full smile that changes the lids and cheek contour. A shows a quieter asymmetric brow lift. Make the lip shape readable inside the beard mass.

## Hair and grooming

Shaved scalp, with subtle tonal variation where short growth is visible. Dark short connected beard and mustache, tidy through the cheeks and fuller along the jaw, with fine irregular boundaries.

## Body and posture

Broad, athletic upper torso and strong arms, with an ordinary waist and substantial legs. Build a balanced firefighter physique from relaxed and shirtless shots, then keep a grounded resting stance.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Dark short-sleeve tee with a small light chest emblem and patterned deep magenta Bayon Buff. B places it at the neck with dark knee-length bottoms in shade. A shows it over the crown in a separate shirtless state. The precise chest insignia and primary shorts color under neutral light need a clearer view.

## Tattoos and accessories

A shows a dark marking on the wearer’s right shoulder at the left edge of frame. It is a candidate tattoo cue with an incomplete crop. Exact motif and extent remain unverified. Record the location and request an unobstructed image before painting.

## Modeling priorities

Read his broad build, shaved scalp, smile and close beard without a Buff. Block the beard as restrained volume and paint density into skin. Avoid treating the shirt’s light emblem as a large graphic.

## Existing model corrections

The stock red tank and cobalt cloth are the wrong outfit for Cambodia. The face reads clean-shaven at this scale, and the stock top clips the shoulder silhouette visually. Establish connected beard and natural breadth, swap in Bayon camp kit and leave the shoulder marking gated.
