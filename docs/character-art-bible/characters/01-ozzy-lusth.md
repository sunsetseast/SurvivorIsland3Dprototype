## Appearance choice

His original finalist season establishes the swimmer silhouette and the loose, dark curl pattern the developer requested. The early-to-midgame shirtless island look is the primary state.

Micronesia has a striped tank and a more controlled, shorter-looking hair presentation. Keep that as a later outfit state rather than length guidance for S13. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s16/images/e1/e1tv105.jpg)

## Face and expression

Block a wide upper cheek plane that tapers into a small rounded chin. Keep the strong dark brows, distinct inner sockets, moderate bridge projection, rounded nasal tip and medium-width lips. Compare the closed-mouth three-quarter against the smile, which lifts his cheek shelf and narrows his lids.

## Hair and grooming

Dark brown, loose irregular curls over the temples and ears and down to the nape. Keep the crown lower than the existing render and allow small sun-lightened ends. The growing mustache and chin stubble are sparse and patchy. Hair reads long around the ears for this period, with no shoulder-length mane.

## Body and posture

Lean swimmer with long limbs, defined but narrow waist, developed shoulders without a thick chest and light lower-leg volume. Give his relaxed pose slightly forward shoulders and a mobile, buoyant stance.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Shirtless with charcoal knee-length board shorts and a thin dark cord with a small pendant. The green camouflage crew-neck shirt is an alternate arrival garment visible in A. C shows a colored upper-arm wrap in the lineup. Keep its exact print, placement in other scenes and default footwear open.

## Tattoos and accessories

Pendant is visible. Metal, shape at macro scale and any tattoo inventory remain unverified. Keep the cord snug at the nape and give the pendant a distinct small highlight.

## Modeling priorities

Read the eyebrows, cheek-to-chin taper and temple-to-nape curl silhouette together. Build layered irregular curl clusters with a dark base, then soften highlight breakup and blend the stubble in UV space.

## Existing model corrections

The current PR #32-derived GLB already has the shirtless charcoal shorts, cord, sandals and sparse stubble. Its rounded brow-to-nose transition, narrow even mouth and close, chunky, light-tipped groom flatten the likeness. The PR #33 candidate improves nape and sockets but its cards still overlap as ribbons. Keep the wardrobe and lean limbs, revise primary face planes and hair clumps.
