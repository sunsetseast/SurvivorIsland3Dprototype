## Appearance choice

Her winning season provides a well documented challenge outfit and a face and build distinct from her return. Anchor to the early Hunahpu lineup.

Winners at War preview shows an older face and darker framing hair. Her S40 blue Sele styling is a separate state. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s40/images/e0/e0tv10.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep the strong upper cheek width, tapered lower oval, relatively full lips, dark brows and deep lid contour. Build the brow-to-bridge and nose base independently of her concentrated expression in B. Use C for unguarded cheek and eye shapes.

## Hair and grooming

Long dark brown roots with noticeably lighter brown or golden lengths, slightly wavy when loose. A gathers the hair for competition and B shows it released around the same blue head Buff. Preserve the two-tone mass when tied, with restrained wisps.

## Body and posture

Compact and powerfully athletic with substantial shoulders, upper arms and thighs. Keep a natural waist and grounded hips, plus a solid stance. Avoid stretching her into a long slim mannequin or importing a later-season physique.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Fitted light aqua sleeveless top and high-saturation blue-violet short bottoms, visible together in A, with patterned blue Hunahpu cloth across the forehead. D shows the season’s light purple short-sleeve shirt with a chest graphic and blue cloth as another on-island state. Leave the tank brand, shorts fabric details and shoes unverified where occluded.

## Tattoos and accessories

A small bright wrist detail is low-resolution. Exact jewelry and tattoo inventory remain open. The blue Buff is the readable accessory, with hue and fold direction more important than tiny lettering.

## Modeling priorities

Lock shoulder-to-hip breadth and two-tone hair first. Read the aqua and violet blocks under daylight, preserve cheek width and distinct lids, and test shoulder seam clearance in paddle and climb poses.

## Existing model corrections

The olive tank, warm neck cloth and purple shorts have the wrong season hierarchy. The slick dark hair obscures the longer highlighted lengths, and the body reads slim through shoulders and legs. Rebuild the wider cheek plane and sturdy proportions, then fit the aqua and blue-violet set with forehead Buff.
