## Face and expression

Keep broad full cheeks, a round lower silhouette, soft chin and a broad nose base. Put the smile into the cheek pads and lower lids, with characteristic upper cheek lift, rather than permanently enlarging the eyes or pulling back the lips.

## Hair and grooming

Dark braids or twists kept close at the scalp, gathered low at the back in B and D. Preserve compact rear volume and neat forward hairline. The high bun of later-season footage is outside this look. Exact braid count is unverified.

## Body and posture

Fuller torso, hips, upper arms and thighs with natural transitions around elbows and knees. Preserve the breadth and forward balance in motion and the soft shoulder slope when seated. Use neither a slim body under loose clothes nor a caricature.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Turquoise longline scoop-neck tank with narrow tied straps and layered multicolor trim at the neckline. A shows the blue patterned Heroes Buff at the forehead. D shows it at the wrist in camp. Dark brief-like shorts or swim bottoms under the long hem are visible in D. The exact cut and fabric need a standing rear or side frame. Bare feet are visible in the challenge state.

## Tattoos and accessories

Small earrings and a bracelet are visible in the camp views. The narrow dark necklace cord is visible in D. Match locations, keep metal or bead construction provisional. No tattoo design was verified.

## Modeling priorities

Read the turquoise neckline, low gather and body proportions from the normal camera. Model the tied strap knots and loose drape so the hem follows hips without looking painted on. Check the open smile as an expression shape.

## Existing model corrections

Production already uses turquoise but has wide simple straps, orange head wrap, camo trousers, high bun and a slimmer cut than S20. The S34-based study adds mass but uses the high knot and light cover, with shallow braid grooves and tight fabric. Fit the S20 tie-strap tank and full body, replace headwear and sculpt low braids, then verify the lower layer.
