## Appearance choice

S40 directly verifies the requested collared red shirt, including its fold and placket. The S20 winning look uses a red scoop-neck tee and shorter hair. Use the S40 Episode 2 challenge lineup as the primary outfit, while preserving the original Pearl Islands roster label.

Heroes vs. Villains opening confessional shows the red scoop-neck tee, necklace and shorter loose hair. S34’s red tee and camo cap are a further alternate, also distinct from the S40 polo. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s20/images/e1r/e1tv13.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep broad cheeks, defined arched brows, a compact nose with broad tip and a short taper into a softly rounded chin. Preserve adult under-eye and nasolabial shape plus her subtle asymmetric smile. Use the camp face without cap shadow to place brow and cheek planes.

## Hair and grooming

Dark brown to near-black, naturally textured, gathered back beneath the Buff in A and C, with a small high gathering in B. The camo-cap arrival view shows curls behind the cap. Preserve textured volume and adult hairline. Do not import the shoulder-length S20 cut into this state.

## Body and posture

Compact, grounded build with natural fullness through upper arms, waist, hips and thighs. Keep ordinary neck and shoulder slopes. The standing lineup is the volume anchor, with expression in the face and stance.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Bright deep-red polo with folded collar, short button placket and short sleeves. A shows it with olive-gray short bottoms, low dark shoes and the red-orange patterned Dakal Buff at the forehead. D shows a same-season alternative with tan trousers, a camouflage cap and the Buff at the wrist. Use A for the head and lower-body choice. Logo, exact fabric blend and hardware are unverified.

## Tattoos and accessories

Small studs are visible in C. The game should capture the polo collar and red-orange Buff placement before tiny patterns. No tattoo design was verified for this set.

## Modeling priorities

The silhouette needs a readable fold collar and sleeve hem at gameplay distance. Match compact adult proportions, dark gathered hair and facial asymmetry in the picker. Keep red distinct from berry and retain the two-button-area placket silhouette without inventing lettering.

## Existing model corrections

Production uses a berry tank, blue wrap, high stock bun and generic short cargo outfit. The Blender study improves asymmetry and cheek form but retains the berry tank and blue wrap and uses long ribbon-like waves. Build the red polo, correct the S40 body volumes and Buff, and derive hair and facial planes from S40.
