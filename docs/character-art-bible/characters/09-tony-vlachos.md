## Appearance choice

The Cagayan winner’s early Brawn challenge set shows the haircut, face and distinctive shoulder ink together. The gray tee and olive cargos are a documented camp variant, with Winners at War kept as a separate aging study.

Winners at War shows an older face and sleeveless gray camp top. The later heavier beard in S40 footage is a separate state. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s40/images/e1/e1tv3.jpg)

## Face and expression

Keep a broad upper skull over a compact face, prominent nose bridge and tip, deep-set lids and a compact chin. Preserve the crease beside the mouth and the asymmetry of his concentrated look without making a permanent scowl.

## Hair and grooming

Closely shaved scalp with faint tonal stubble. Close dark beard shadow along the mouth, jaw and chin with some uneven density. Preserve the short hair-to-skin transition instead of a smooth uniform peach scalp.

## Body and posture

Compact, strong torso with substantial neck, shoulders and forearms. A natural center of mass and animated forward gesture read better than a hero-wide shoulder span or cut abdominal shapes.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Primary Brawn challenge state is shirtless with bright red shorts carrying pale edge details, verified in B. The gray crew-neck short-sleeve tee and olive or khaki cargo trousers in C are an early camp state. Keep shorts brand and footwear detail open.

## Tattoos and accessories

Dark sweeping tattoo on the wearer’s left shoulder, curving onto upper chest and around deltoid. A and D establish placement, scale and main negative spaces. Obtain lateral and rear contours before drawing the full wrap. Orange Brawn Buff appears in separate team moments. No persistent placement is established for the selected challenge frame.

## Modeling priorities

Keep the same shaved-head silhouette at camera distance and make the upper-left tattoo read as one continuous anchored design. Model cloth drape for the alternate instead of putting the tattoo on top of a tee.

## Existing model corrections

The gray sleeveless proxy and fatigue trousers hide the bright challenge silhouette. The production scalp is smooth, face generic and tattoo missing in the exposed area. Keep the bald cue, reconstruct bridge and compact jaw, add stubble and left-side ink, and use red shorts.
