## Appearance choice

The winner’s original Puka and Aitu footage gives a younger, lean outline and cleaner jaw than Winners at War. Use the early pale tank and dark shorts, with the Puka visor as a removable state.

Winners at War has more facial hair, an older facial structure and red styling. Keep its palette separate. [Comparison image](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s40/images/e0/e0tv63.jpg)

## Face and expression

Build a long face with a broad cheek plane, a straight brow and a firm lower jaw that tapers toward the chin. Preserve the narrower lid openings, nose projection in three-quarter and subtle asymmetry of his smile. Keep the upper lip and jaw contours readable with or without stubble.

## Hair and grooming

Short upright dark crop with close sides and an uneven front edge. Dark growth on the upper lip and chin varies in S13. Use light visible stubble where supported by C rather than the more established S40 beard.

## Body and posture

Relatively tall athletic frame with long limbs and distinct shoulders. Keep the torso upright and balanced, with moderate shoulder-to-waist taper and a composed stance. His silhouette should have more length and structure than Ozzy’s.

## Clothing, Buff and footwear

Pale off-white to lavender-gray scoop tank and dark above-knee shorts. D shows the pairing at Aitu. C documents a cream fabric visor with dark underside in an earlier Puka state. Leave the default visor off for the Aitu view, and reserve any green Puka Buff detail for a matching observed shot.

## Tattoos and accessories

C confirms visor color and brim shape. No signature jewelry or ink verified. Exact shorts hem features, footwear and small logos remain open.

## Modeling priorities

Read straight brow, lid contour and long jaw on an unlit gray pass, then check upright frame with pale tank at gameplay distance. Keep visor low profile and distinguish it from a full cap.

## Existing model corrections

The black training tank and blue shorts reverse the documented light-over-dark contrast. The crop is blocky and the face loses lid and jaw differences. Keep the upright long-limbed silhouette, revise planes, then fit the pale top and dark shorts.
