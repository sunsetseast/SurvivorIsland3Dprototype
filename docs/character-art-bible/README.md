## Reading the evidence

**Verified** means visible in a named frame or explicitly reported by an attributed source. **Probable** means a reading of proportions, surface or fit that varies with pose, lighting or camera. **Unverified** means the available crop, lighting or view cannot establish the detail. Treat athlete builds, exact body measurements, tattoo line art and clothing logos as separate decisions that need their own evidence.

The selected appearance is an art-direction choice. It can differ from the original roster season. All 18 IDs and original identities are mapped at baseline `3b37dcd5daf85583eff83a4650c212d1d070efc2`. The read-only 2D portraits were reviewed for identity continuity. Each alternate appearance is kept separate from the selected outfit and grooming.

Phase 1 covers the documents and linked evidence. The next approval is for the reference choices and gap list, before a separate production phase.
