## Broadcast, press and episode stills

CBS and Paramount Press Express supply official photography, captions and season context. The cast boards also use CBS broadcast stills from True Dork Times, an independent archive. The archive’s episode frame and caption establish where each image comes from. Intro frames can be heavily graded, so use daylight camp or challenge frames for wardrobe and skin color. In a group shot, the board identifies the contestant by position where practical. Press links identify the intended image by filename in case the gallery moves.
