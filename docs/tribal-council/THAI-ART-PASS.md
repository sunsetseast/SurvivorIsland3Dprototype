# Thai-inspired sanctuary art pass

This branch adds original geometry inspired by historic Ayutthaya and Sukhothai temple forms. The sanctuary geography, original simulation separation, contestant seating and existing exploratory controls remain unchanged.

## Additions

- Three layered background chedis with ringed bases and tapering spires.
- Two elephant-shaped guardian statues framed behind the Council.
- A ceremonial naga-balustrade entrance section with low stone steps and a short bridge.
- A tiered pagoda canopy over the existing private voting chamber, keeping its dogleg sightline restriction and writing table.
- More legible paved voting-route markers, flanking torches and localized warm light.
- Raised visual framing and torchlight at the jury gallery.
- Standing ornamental stone host dais replacing the previous wooden chair; existing host and urn/snuffer anchors stay fixed.

## Verification status

These are direct GitHub source changes, not an exported or rendered 3D asset pack. A browser WebGL build, TypeScript compile, visual screenshots, path-collision regression and actual iPhone Safari performance have **not yet been run** for this branch. Review geometry layering, path paving direction, roof clearance, sightlines, camera obstruction and cast positions before merging. The step/bridge details are visual surfacing over the existing walkable terrain, rather than new physically stepped navigation elevation. Do not represent the work as production certified.
