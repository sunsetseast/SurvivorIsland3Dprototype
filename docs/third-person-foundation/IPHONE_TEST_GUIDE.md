# iPhone test (landscape)

Open the development build containing this draft PR on your iPhone.

1. Continue Camp. Move the left joystick slowly, fully and diagonally; toggle Run. Release it. Turns and stops should feel smooth.
2. Keep the left thumb moving; drag empty scenery on the right with the other thumb. Look behind and up/down. Movement should keep its direction until you release the joystick. Stop dragging; camera returns behind after about 1.6 seconds.
3. Menu → Challenge Testing → Coastal Traverse → Show controls → Ready. Open stretches run automatically. Crawl, beam and net select their own appropriate speed and pose; no Run tap is needed.
4. At the low frame, center the lane and tap Enter low crawl. Hold forward. Look from the side: body bends at joints, head retains its size, arms and legs alternate. Continue until standing outside the far end.
5. Mount the beam. Hold forward and steer toward the center of the balance indicator. Fall once: see the drop/return/rise, then retry the crossing.
6. Climb net: alternate Left/Right hand steadily, watching ascent, crest and descent. Collect the three bundles; assemble the puzzle. Camera should leave inspection after completion.
7. Pause/resume, Retry and return through Challenge Hub. Try Stillwater Balance, then return to your preserved camp. Repeat Low and High if practical.

Report which stage, quality and gesture felt wrong, plus a short iPhone recording if available. Browser emulation and CI evidence do not certify physical Safari feel or FPS.
