# Coastal Traverse rules

Rehearsal only: no immunity, permanent result, season elimination or social changes.

Two existing lanes support sequential two-person heats. The player races first; remaining actual contestants compete in later visible heats. Personal completion time determines overall places, including recovery waits. Exact fixed-step ties share places. Unfinished four-minute heats are DNF. Pause freezes every contestant and clock.

1. **Sprint:** push the existing joystick forward along your marked lane. Center before mounting obstacles; you cannot bypass a stage. Backward movement stays within the current segment so the standing pose cannot re-enter completed equipment.
2. **Crawl:** tap Enter low crawl, then keep moving through the frame in the low-profile pose.
3. **Beam:** move forward and steer left/right to counter predictable drift. Neutral forward steering can fail. Large imbalance or depleted stability resets only the beam and holds movement for two seconds; correct steering restores stability.
4. **Net:** alternate Left hand / Right hand steadily. Rapid repeats are suppressed, repeated hands and long waits cause small slips. Body position follows the actual A-frame continuously.
5. **Collection:** move beside each of three bundles on your finish deck and tap Collect bundle. Each provides two pieces.
6. **Karst Mosaic:** drag six authored geometric carvings into their correct slots. Wrong slots reject pieces. Reset returns pieces to the tray. Tap-piece/tap-slot and keyboard/pointer alternatives are available. Only six validated placements finish the race; no automatic solution or fake completion timer exists.

Keyboard: W/S or arrows for forward/back; A/D or arrows to steer; Space for contextual actions; Q/E for net hands; Escape to pause. Puzzle buttons support Tab/Enter/Space. UI-focused Space activates its UI action once.

Opponents use copied existing strength/endurance/dexterity/focus traits and independent seeded stage/puzzle attempts. Physical pace creates an advantage, but puzzle performance can reverse the order. No permanent mental/fatigue statistics are invented. Waiting contestants stage outside the lanes between heats; there is no overlapping crowd or overtaking system.
