# iPhone test guide

Use code from draft PR #26, branch `feat/coastal-traverse`, in your existing development preview. GitHub changes do not automatically prove that another project's preview updated. No hosted settings or Replit tools were accessed.

1. Open your usual development preview and tap **Challenge Testing** on welcome.
2. Find **Coastal Traverse · Playable** and tap **Play Coastal Traverse**. If it says Environment Preview, the preview is older code. Check the selected/synced branch and welcome Build revision. Production builds intentionally have no testing hub.
3. Turn your phone sideways. Read the host introduction, tap **Show controls**, then **Ready · Race**.
4. Use the lower-left joystick to run. Center in your lane and tap the obstacle action. Keep moving through the crawl.
5. On the beam, keep moving forward while gently steering toward zero balance. A fall costs two seconds at that checkpoint.
6. Alternate **Left hand / Right hand** on the net.
7. Move beside all three finish-deck bundles and tap **Collect bundle**.
8. Drag the six carvings to matching mosaic slots, or tap a piece then a slot. **Reset board** starts the puzzle again.
9. Watch remaining timed heats, then use **Retry race**, **Challenge Hub** or **Welcome**. **Continue Survivor Session** resumes the original camp and pose.

**Pause** freezes all race clocks. Resume explicitly after switching away from the browser. For Low quality, open an environment preview, use its Quality button, return to Hub and select Coastal again.

Once this branch is running, play requires no MacBook, terminal, console or URL edits. Selecting/syncing the draft branch depends on your existing project's source-control interface; no production bypass is provided. Physical iPhone Safari and the live hosted preview were not tested; please check notch/home-indicator reachability and dragging comfort on your phone.
