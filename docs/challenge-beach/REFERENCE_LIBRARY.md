# Challenge reference library

Researched 2026-11-05. These are actual televised challenge designs, not claims that gameplay is implemented. Fan archives document mechanics/history; the production designer's portfolio and creator interview provide primary construction context. Some Fandom pages refuse direct retrieval; indexed content was cross-checked with the accessible episode archives below. Episode numbering differs for combined finales, so named finales are retained where appropriate. No uncertain episode numbers are invented.

All dimensions below are **original game-layout estimates in metres**, including circulation, not measured television set dimensions. Photos remain on their original pages; no CBS stills, branding, logos or copyrighted decorative artwork are redistributed. Each photo link opens a source page containing the relevant still/gallery, rather than an invented image URL.

## Geographic sources and construction decisions

- [Thai Ministry of Tourism directory: Railay](https://www.thailandtourismdirectory.go.th/en/attraction/1805): limestone-separated west/east beach identities, fine sand, clear west-side water and eastern climbing coast. Use a curved west-facing strand, enclosing karst headlands and inland vegetation. Do not turn Railay East into a duplicate broad swimming beach.
- [Tourism Authority of Thailand brochure](https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/uK8Kxy2oDV/E_Brochure_TAT_2024_English_Version.pdf): visual/geographic context for limestone and beach composition. Its compressed attraction prose is not used as a precise shoreline survey.
- [Existing geographical database](../../data/railay/README.md): repository OSM source, coordinate convention and attribution; preserve the existing `challengeWest` map anchor. This compact authored scene does not replace the OSM-derived peninsula or claim measured GIS dimensions.
- [Primary creator interview and CBS construction photographs](https://parade.com/tv/50-best-survivor-challenges-ever-interview): modularity and readable human-scale structures inform the original timber/net equipment. No exact TV art is copied.

Research stops at these 15 designs. Main and jungle courses use the common obstacle/retrieval/puzzle principles; the ocean relay is visibly complete only when its model checkpoint lands, and cannot imply swimming exists. The individual model combines generic balance, overhead endurance and stacking components. Only four demonstration configurations will be authored, one active at a time.
